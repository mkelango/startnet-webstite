# NIRF Summit, Investors Summit and other StartNet events: public-source research

Research date: 13 Oct 2026. Read-only web research; no logins. Every fact carries its source. "WB" = Wayback Machine
capture (the live site is gone). "ID-derived" = date decoded from the post's numeric ID (X / LinkedIn snowflake), not
printed on the page. Items marked **[snippet only]** come from a search-engine result snippet; the page itself could not
be opened (LinkedIn returns HTTP 999, X returns 402), so treat them as unconfirmed.

Already on the site (not repeated here): GenAI Connect 2024, D2C Connect 2024, SaaS Connect 2025, Vibathon 2025, Vibe
Marketing Summit 2025, the placeholder `investors-summit-archive` (`server/data/events.json`), and `founders-day`
(`proposed`, `seriesData.js`).

## 1. NIRF Higher Ed Summit 2024 (StartNet, two editions)

**Verdict: confirmed as StartNet's.** The Salem summit's own website carries StartNet's logo, names M. K. Elango as
"Founder & CEO, StartNet", gives `info@startnet.in` as the contact, links to the @startnetindia social accounts, and
has the footer "Copyright @ Startnet Ventures Private Limited 2024". The Coimbatore edition is credited by Amrita to
"StartNet Research", whose own site (startnetresearch.in) has the footer "© 2024 Startnet Ventures Private Limited" and
lists M.K. Elango as Founder & CEO.

The summit is about India's National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF): a one-day event for college and university
leaders on improving their NIRF ranking.
