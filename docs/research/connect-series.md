# Connect series: web research

Researched and accessed on **13 Oct 2026**. The scope was GenAI Connect, D2C Connect, SaaS Connect and any other StartNet "<sector> Connect". These notes cover only what is **new or different** from what the site already has in `server/data/events.json`, `client/src/pages/mega/events-series/seriesData.js`, `client/src/data/videos.js` and `docs/VIDEOS.md`.

## How to read the sources

- **Kind of source.** Each fact is tagged by where it came from:
  - **OFFICIAL**: the event's own site or channel.
  - **OFFICIAL-IDX**: the event's own site, read from the search engine's index because the site is now offline. `genaiconnect.startnet.in` and `d2cconnect.in` no longer resolve, and `saasconnect.in` now serves a gambling page.
  - **PARTNER**: a sponsor or partner.
  - **3P**: a third party.
  - **AGG**: an aggregator listing.
- **OFFICIAL-IDX text is paraphrased.** The search tool returns a summary of the page, not the page itself, so these facts are close paraphrases. Some speaker and agenda blocks were "jumbled" in the index. Re-check exact wording against an archive copy or StartNet's own files before publishing. The Wayback Machine was blocked to this tool.
- **Pre-event numbers are targets.** Most figures are pre-event marketing ("2500+ attendees"). They are labelled as projections unless the source states them after the event.
- **Sources that could not be reached:**
  - Blocked to this tool: The Hindu, Times of India, New Indian Express and Dinamani.
  - Returned 403 or 999: LinkedIn post pages, Business Wire, 10times and the JesperApps registration page.
  - No coverage found on DT Next, YourStory, Inc42, Dinamalar or Covai Post for any edition.
- **Roles.** Speakers are named only with the role the source gives. Names listed without a role are marked "role not stated" and should not be published as-is.
