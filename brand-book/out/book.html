How to use this book

What this is

The single source for how StartNet looks and sounds. Where this book and an older file disagree, this book is correct.

It builds on the brand system in the StartNet Website Books (Book 3, Brand Identity) and adopts SNX Red as the primary colour.

What it governs

Anything carrying the name: the website, StartNet X, decks, print, events, signage, social, merchandise and partner material.

It covers all four talent brands: Research, Vibe, Connect and Women. Product interface tokens inherit from here.

When it is silent

Follow the nearest example in the book, and send the work to brand review before it ships.

A new pattern used twice becomes a rule. Write it in here rather than repeating the judgement.

01.7

Writing rules

  1. 01StartNet is one word, capital S and N.The red full stop belongs to the logo. In running text, StartNet takes no dot.
  2. 02Numerals use Indian grouping.1,00,000, not 100,000. Write ₹6 Cr and ₹6 கோடி, in Indo-Arabic digits in both languages.
  3. 03Every figure travels with its proof.Source, A/B/C tag and as-at date, in one unit: [value] founders · [A] · as at [date].
  4. 04Tamil is written natively, not translated.Tamil editors write it. No letter-spacing and no all-caps styling on Tamil.
  5. 05Calls to action start with a verb.Join StartNet · Find a programme · Read the Ledger. Tamil CTAs stay under about 18 characters.
  6. 06Tamil Nadu is two words.Spell out GCC (Global Capability Centre) on first use.
  7. 07No exclamation marks, no superlatives.Short declaratives that end on the outcome.
  8. 08British English, sentence case.Programme, organisation, colour. Headlines and buttons in sentence case.

Colour versions

StartNet
Primary

Ink wordmark, red full stop, on Paper or white. First choice everywhere. The stop is 3.39:1 on Paper, above the 3:1 for graphics.

StartNet
Reversed

Paper wordmark, red full stop, on Ink, night surfaces and dark photography. The stop is 4.84:1 on Ink.

StartNet
Mono Ink

Ink wordmark and stop. Single-colour print, embossing, mono partner material, and Vibe or Connect fills.

StartNet
Mono White

White wordmark and stop. Research, Women or red fills, dark photographs with busy colour, low-grade print.

Grounds that are allowed

Primary on Paper, white and light photography. Reversed on Ink, night surfaces and dark photography.

Never colour on an accent

On an accent, red or mid-tone photo the red stop disappears. Use mono: the stop keeps its shape and gives up its colour.

Beside imagery, not over it

Set the logo on a solid field next to the image. Where it must sit on a photograph, use a calm area; busy colour takes Mono White.

03.2

How the palette is derived

OKLCH hue
One axis,
two directions
SNX Red · 25°the given
Marigold · 70°analogous, +45°
Ink · Lagoon · 205°complement, 180° across
  1. 01Secondary: take the complement to near-black.Ink #0C1719 sits 180° across the wheel at lightness 0.20. It reads as black, yet its blue-green bias makes the red look warmer and brighter beside it.
  2. 02Tertiary, cool: lift the same complement.Lagoon #117780 is Ink's lighter sibling (lightness 0.52), so the cool tertiary is part of the same family rather than a new hue.
  3. 03Tertiary, warm: step 45° toward yellow.Marigold #FBA62A is the red's analogous neighbour at high lightness: warm and celebratory, the colour of a marigold garland.
Considered and rejected

Blue-violet, 265°. Too close to Research and the data blue.

Rose, 0°. Reads as Women and muddies the red.

Green, 145°. Collides with Connect and "success".

Its width axis (75–125) keeps a long Tamil headline to two lines without dropping the size. If it misses the first render, Noto stands in.

Eight rules for Tamil
  1. 01Body one step up, display one step down.Body ×1.06 with about 16% more leading; display ×0.82–0.9, as headlines run 30–40% longer.
  2. 02No letter-spacing.Tracking breaks conjuncts. All tracking resets to 0 in Tamil.
  3. 03No all-caps styling.No case in Tamil. Eyebrows: 14 px, weight 600, no transform.
  4. 04Never split inside a syllable.Animate by word or line; never word-break: break-all.
  5. 05Indian digit grouping, no Tamil numerals.1,00,000 in both languages. Write ₹6 கோடி; never ௧, ௨ in UI or data.
  6. 06Brand names stay Latin.“StartNet X” stays in Inter inside Tamil text, so it looks the same in both.
  7. 07Weights 400 and 600.Avoid 300 and below: hairline Tamil strokes break up on low-DPI screens.
  8. 08Set the lang attributes.lang="ta" on Tamil pages and lang="en" on English spans inside them.

The full stop system

One red point per composition. If two things are red, neither is the point.

  1. 01Always a true circle.Never stretched, outlined, shaded or glowing.
  2. 02Red or mono only.SNX Red, or mono on accent fills and in one-colour print.
  3. 03Never two red points.Other emphasis takes Ink or the brand accent.
  4. 04Never inside the logo’s clear space.Keep 3d clear around any lock-up.
  5. 05Only the full stop moves.It lands once per session; letters stay still.
