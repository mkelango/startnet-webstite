01.1

Mission and promise

Promise

Every talent,
a founder

The full stop is deliberate: a statement, not a hope. It is where a founder’s story turns from “maybe” into “done”: the company registered, the pilot signed, the first ₹ raised.

Positioning

Tamil Nadu’s founder-making network

It replaces “India’s first integrated startup ecosystem platform”.

Mission

We go to campuses, labs, workplaces and districts, and help people with the talent to build turn it into companies.

As written on startnet.in.

Goal · Mission 1 Lakh
1,00,000founders nurtured in
Tamil Nadu by March 2031

Counted in a public, quarterly Founder Ledger. “Nurtured” means a StartNet X profile plus at least two meaningful programme touchpoints (L2 and above). A planning target, not a result.

01.7

Writing rules

  1. 01StartNet is one word, capital S and N.The red full stop belongs to the logo. In running text, StartNet takes no dot.
  2. 02Numerals use Indian grouping.1,00,000, not 100,000. Write ₹6 Cr and ₹6 கோடி, in Indo-Arabic digits in both languages.
  3. 03Every figure travels with its proof.Source, A/B/C tag and as-at date, in one unit: [value] founders · [A] · as at [date].
  4. 04Tamil is written natively, not translated.Tamil editors write it. No letter-spacing and no all-caps styling on Tamil.
  5. 05Calls to action start with a verb.Join StartNet · Find a programme · Read the Ledger. Tamil CTAs stay under about 18 characters.
  6. 06Tamil Nadu is two words.Spell out GCC (Global Capability Centre) on first use.
  7. 07No exclamation marks, no superlatives.Short declaratives that end on the outcome.
  8. 08British English, sentence case.Programme, organisation, colour. Headlines and buttons in sentence case.

StartNet is Tamil Nadu’s founder-making network. Its four brands, StartNet Research, StartNet Vibe, StartNet Connect and StartNet Women, help scholars, students, professionals and women become founders through events, learning, incubation, capital and market access, all run on one platform, StartNet X. StartNet’s goal, Mission 1 Lakh, is to nurture 1,00,000 founders in Tamil Nadu by March 2031, counted in a public, quarterly Founder Ledger. StartNet is based in Salem, Tamil Nadu. Every talent, a founder.
