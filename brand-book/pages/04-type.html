Its width axis (75–125) keeps a long Tamil headline to two lines without dropping the size. If it misses the first render, Noto stands in.

Eight rules for Tamil
  1. 01Body one step up, display one step down.Body ×1.06 with about 16% more leading; display ×0.82–0.9, as headlines run 30–40% longer.
  2. 02No letter-spacing.Tracking breaks conjuncts. All tracking resets to 0 in Tamil.
  3. 03No all-caps styling.No case in Tamil. Eyebrows: 14 px, weight 600, no transform.
  4. 04Never split inside a syllable.Animate by word or line; never word-break: break-all.
  5. 05Indian digit grouping, no Tamil numerals.1,00,000 in both languages. Write ₹6 கோடி; never ௧, ௨ in UI or data.
  6. 06Brand names stay Latin.“StartNet X” stays in Inter inside Tamil text, so it looks the same in both.
  7. 07Weights 400 and 600.Avoid 300 and below: hairline Tamil strokes break up on low-DPI screens.
  8. 08Set the lang attributes.lang="ta" on Tamil pages and lang="en" on English spans inside them.
04.3

Type scale and rules

  1. 01Line length 60–72 characters.Set English body to 68ch. Cap Tamil at 36 em, since its ch unit misleads.
  2. 02Tabular figures in data.Every table, counter, date column and price, so figures never jitter.
  3. 03Sentence case.Headlines, buttons and labels. Capitals only for print labels.
  4. 04Ranged left, ragged right.No justified text, no centred paragraphs.
  5. 05Two sizes per element.A card, tile or slide block takes one head size and one text size.

04.4

The full stop system

One red point per composition. If two things are red, neither is the point.

  1. 01Always a true circle.Never stretched, outlined, shaded or glowing.
  2. 02Red or mono only.SNX Red, or mono on accent fills and in one-colour print.
  3. 03Never two red points.Other emphasis takes Ink or the brand accent.
  4. 04Never inside the logo’s clear space.Keep 3d clear around any lock-up.
  5. 05Only the full stop moves.It lands once per session; letters stay still.

Principles
  1. 01Real Tamil Nadu.Workshops, campuses, labs and districts, as they are.
  2. 02Doing, not posing.Hands on the work, a pitch mid-sentence, a circle in discussion.
  3. 03Proof in the caption.Name, organisation, place and date. Evidence is never a composite.

Allowed with a label: abstract textures and concept illustrations, captioned “AI-assisted illustration”, directed by a named designer and logged.
