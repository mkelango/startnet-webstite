FAQ

What professionals ask before they start.

Do I have to leave my job?

No. Founder Switch runs on evenings and weekends for 12 weeks, so you can test your idea and find a co-founder before you leave your job. You finish with a go or no-go decision you can defend.

Isn't this just networking without outcomes?

Each Connect is a community, not a single date. In Connect Circles, introductions and referrals are logged on StartNet X and reported back to members. Founder outcomes are counted in the Founder Ledger with an A/B/C confidence tag and an as-at date.

What does it cost?

Most first events are free. Connect Circles membership is from ₹30,000 a year [C] and includes StartNet X Pro. The Founder Switch fee is [placeholder — rate card; indicative ₹35,000–75,000] [C]. Some GCCs sponsor seats for their employees.

Why is there only one seat per sector in a Circle?

So members refer business instead of competing for it. Membership is by application. We'll check your sector seat is free before you pay.

Who runs a Connect Circle?

Volunteer chairs, with StartNet running operations. Circles meet monthly.

My startup already has revenue. Is Connect for me?

Yes. Scale Connect is for startups past first revenue: curated investor days, pilot matchmaking with GCCs and corporates, readiness for government procurement, and export desks connected to Tamil diaspora networks. It is included with StartNet X Scale.

My sector isn't running yet. What now?

GenAI, SaaS and D2C Connect are running. DeepTech, FinTech and Industry 4.0 Connect are proposed for 2027; HealthTech, AgriFood and Climate Connect are proposed, with dates to be set. Register interest on the sector's page and we'll tell you when it opens.

Can I invest through StartNet Angels today?

No. StartNet Angels is not yet open for investment. Nothing on this page is an offer of securities or investment advice. Investing in early-stage companies carries a high risk of loss. Participation will be limited to eligible investors through a registered structure.
