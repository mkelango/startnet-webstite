Who Vibe is for

Kavya, 20, third-year ECE

An illustrative persona, not a real student. She studies at a Tier 3 college in Virudhunagar, builds with AI tools on a mid-range Android and prefers Tamil for explanations.

What she needs
A free first step; skills that lead to a job or a company.
What she does first
Registers for Vibathon or Vibe Campus, starts Vibe Academy, joins a Vibe Club.
Where she hears about it
Instagram and YouTube, WhatsApp forwards, her college’s Innovation Council, search.

Fair questions, straight answers

Is this a fee trap?

Most first steps are free. Vibe Campus and Vibe Clubs cost nothing. Vibathon is free to ₹499 per team , and fee waivers are available on request.

Only for Chennai?

No. Vibathon starts with district qualifiers, and 80% of the colleges Vibathon 2025 reached were Tier 3 or 4 .

My English isn’t good enough.

You can learn in Tamil or English, and present your Vibathon build in either.
