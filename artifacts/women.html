Questions

What women founders ask first.

Is StartNet Women only for tech founders?

No. StartNet Women helps women build product and service companies. It is for professional women, including women returning after a career break; business owners in home-based, MSME and self-help-group enterprises; and researchers and students in StartNet Research and Vibe programmes.

Is my business too small?

No. Business owners in home-based, MSME and self-help-group enterprises who want to grow into product or service companies are exactly who we design for. We put market access first: buyer introductions and supplier-diversity programmes, because revenue makes founders investable.

Is the first step free?

Talent never hits a paywall first. Most first steps are free, and a free StartNet X profile follows you across every programme. Circle membership and Academy fees are listed with each programme.

Can I take part in Tamil?

Yes. District Circles are Tamil-first, and Rise Academy runs in Tamil or English.

What happens at a Women Circle?

A chapter of 20–30 women founders meets monthly for accountability, problem-solving and introductions to buyers.

What does a Circle cost?

Membership is from ₹10,000 a year. This is indicative [C] until the rate card is published. CSR-funded places are available [placeholder]. Circle members get StartNet X Pro included.

When do sessions happen?

We run daytime and weekend sessions. Childcare at flagship events: [placeholder — confirm before publishing].

What does Rise Academy cost?

The fee is [placeholder — CSR-subsidised; indicative ₹5,000–15,000] [C]. Ask about sponsored seats.

Does StartNet decide on grants and loans?

No. We help you prepare and apply. Decisions on grants and loans are made by the scheme, not by StartNet.

Which schemes do you help with?

Schemes for women-led businesses, including StartupTN's TANSEED (up to ₹15 L for women-led startups), TIIC's Vetri Magalir loans (₹10 L to ₹5 Cr) and NITI Aayog's Women Entrepreneurship Platform. Amounts are as published by each scheme [B]; verify before applying. Open the scheme explorer.

How do you count “women-led” and “women-owned”?

DPIIT counts a startup as women-led if it has at least one woman director. We also report “women-owned” as 51%+ equity held by women, a stricter count.

Is there a women angel circle?

A women angel circle is planned [proposed]. It is not open yet.
